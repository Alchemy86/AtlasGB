# The Pokédex — Pokémon Red/Blue

[← Pokémon Red/Blue](../README.md) · [by address](by-address.md) · [by name](by-name.md) · [the structures](structures.md) · [AtlasGB](../../../README.md)

Two flag arrays and nothing else: nineteen bytes each, one bit per Pokédex number, for 151
species. `wPokedexSeen` is set by the battle engine the first time a species appears;
`wPokedexOwned` when you catch it.

The relationship between them is one of the atlas's better checks. **You cannot own a Pokémon
you have never seen**, so every bit of `wPokedexOwned` must also be set in `wPokedexSeen`. A
claim about two addresses at once is much harder to satisfy by accident than a claim about
either alone, and it is verified against a real save on every run.
