# The union area — Pokémon Red/Blue

[← Pokémon Red/Blue](../README.md) · [by address](by-address.md) · [by name](by-name.md) · [the structures](structures.md) · [AtlasGB](../../../README.md)

`$CBFC-$D162` is 357 entries and it is not a subsystem. It is **bytes that a dozen unrelated
routines take turns owning**: the slot machine, the Hall of Fame, the credits, the trade menus,
the Pokédex rating, a battle animation's scratch, a text buffer. `$CC5B` alone has eight
different names.

That is deliberate on the game's part — work RAM is 8 KiB and most of these things can never be
happening at once — and it is the single most dangerous region for a reader. **An address here
means nothing without knowing which routine is live.** A tool that samples one of these bytes
on a timer and reports it will produce a number that is real, stable and about something else
entirely.

The entries are listed with their aliases so that the overlap is visible rather than implied.
Naming the *owners* of each union rather than its members would be a better page than this one,
and nobody has written it.

### Findings behind these bytes

| the finding | the bytes it is about |

| [a critical hit is the worst case](discoveries.md#a-critical-hit-is-the-worst-case) | [`wMonHBaseSpeed`](#s-wMonHBaseSpeed) |
| [the battle move menu cursor is 1-based](discoveries.md#the-battle-move-menu-cursor-is-1-based) | [`wPlayerMoveListIndex`](#s-wPlayerMoveListIndex) |

`wPlayerStatsToDouble` is the clearest case of an address mattering for something other than
its name: whatever it is called, it is the **first of the five bytes a switch zeroes**, and
those five take in all three of the side's battle-status bytes. That is why a Generation 1
REFLECT belongs to the Pokémon and not to the side.
