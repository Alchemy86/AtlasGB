# The structures — Pokémon Red/Blue

[← Pokémon Red/Blue](../README.md) · [by address](by-address.md) · [by name](by-name.md) · [the structures](structures.md) · [AtlasGB](../../../README.md)

Five repeated records make up most of the game's state, and every one of these tables is
**derived from the atlas's own addresses** rather than transcribed — the offsets are differences
between symbols, and the sizes are differences between consecutive slots. Two of them are
verified live on every run: the party stride really is 44, the box stride really is 33, and the
battle structure really is 29 bytes with `wTrainerClass` immediately after it.

Read the three Pokémon shapes together, because the differences between them are the point:

* **`party_struct`, 44 bytes** — what you carry. Everything.
* **`box_struct`, 33 bytes** — what the PC stores. The same, minus the level and the five
  computed stats, because a stored Pokémon's stats are recalculated from its experience and DVs
  when it is withdrawn. That is why depositing and withdrawing can change a Pokémon's stats.
* **`battle_struct`, 29 bytes** — what is fighting. Minus the original trainer, the experience
  and the stat experience, so **the level and the five stats are at different offsets from the
  party form**. Confusing the two is how you produce a level 6,400 Pokémon.

Three rules apply to all of them:

1. **Every multi-byte field is big-endian**, on a little-endian console.
2. **The HP DV is not stored.** It is assembled from bit 0 of the other four, most significant
   first — so of the five DVs that decide what a Pokémon is, only four are fields.
3. **`Species` is the internal index**, not the Pokédex number. See [tables in the
   cartridge](rom-data.md).

### `party_struct` — 44 bytes

One of the six Pokemon you carry. Also the shape the link cable sends, and the shape a trainer's team is built in.

Base `wPartyMon1` at `$D16B`.

| offset | bytes | field | notes |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | `Species` | Species **internal index** — not the Pokedex number. `PokedexOrder` converts. |
| 1 | 2 | `HP` | Current HP, **big-endian**. |
| 3 | 1 | `BoxLevel` | The level as stored. Kept in step with `Level` for a party Pokemon; for a stored one it is the only level there is. |
| 4 | 1 | `Status` | Sleep, poison, burn, freeze, paralysis, as one byte of flags and a sleep counter. |
| 5 | 1 | `Type` / `Type1` | The two type bytes as a pair, `Type1` then `Type2`. |
| 6 | 1 | `Type2` | Second type; equal to `Type1` for a single-typed Pokemon. |
| 7 | 1 | `CatchRate` | The species' base catch rate, copied in at capture. Held item in later generations; here it is the ball maths. |
| 8 | 4 | `Moves` | Four move ids, 0 for an empty slot. |
| 12 | 2 | `OTID` | The original trainer's ID, **big-endian**. Compared against `wPlayerID` to decide whether this Pokemon was traded to you. |
| 14 | 3 | `Exp` | Experience, **three bytes big-endian**. |
| 17 | 2 | `HPExp` | Stat experience for HP, big-endian. Gained per battle and folded into the stat by `CalcStat`. |
| 19 | 2 | `AttackExp` | Stat experience for Attack. |
| 21 | 2 | `DefenseExp` | Stat experience for Defense. |
| 23 | 2 | `SpeedExp` | Stat experience for Speed. |
| 25 | 2 | `SpecialExp` | Stat experience for Special. |
| 27 | 2 | `DVs` | Two bytes, four nybbles: attack, defense, speed, special. **The HP DV is not stored** — it is assembled from bit 0 of the other four, most significant first. These sixteen bits are the whole of what makes one Pokemon differ from another of its species and level. |
| 29 | 4 | `PP` | Four bytes: current PP in the low 6 bits, PP Ups applied in the top 2. |
| 33 | 1 | `Level` | The level the stats below were computed at. |
| 34 | 2 | `MaxHP` / `Stats` | Computed maximum HP, big-endian. |
